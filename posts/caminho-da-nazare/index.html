Caminho da Nazaré

Another post where I wanted to write a lot, but it ended up being a dump of beautiful photos from a hike I did with two of my friends, at the beginning of this month. The trail we hiked is known as the Caminho da Nazaré, in the Leiria region, Portugal. It was a 54 km hike that’s normally split into three days, but we did it in two.

Day 1 - Nazaré to Porto de Mós

On the first day, we hiked ~30 km from the coastal town of Nazaré to Porto de Mós.

Day 2 - Porto de Mós to Fátima

The second day was shorter, we hiked ~24 km to reach Fátima.
